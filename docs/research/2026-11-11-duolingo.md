## 2. Message choice (KDD 2020 bandit paper)

Source: https://research.duolingo.com/papers/yancey.kdd20.pdf (Yancey and Settles, KDD 2020, doi 10.1145/3394486.3403351). All CONFIRMED.

- Pool: "The content of these notifications is generated from pools of hand-written templates (which may contain slots for user-specific information) from which a single template is chosen".
- Eligibility: each template has rules, for example needs a streak wager. A template is only a candidate if eligible ("sleeping" arms).
- Reward: "the reward is 1 if the user completes a lesson within two hours of the reminder being sent, and 0 otherwise." They use 2 hours to cut out lessons the user would have done anyway.
- Score: compare reward when the template is sent against reward when it was eligible but another was sent. This controls for who is eligible. Scores are a relative lift (about plus or minus 3 percent). Small-sample scores are shrunk toward a prior (empirical Bayes) because "38 % dubiously have scores outside ±5 %" for arms with little data.
- Novelty: "templates that users had not previously seen generally had positive impact." Recency penalty: s* = s - gamma * 0.5^(d/h), where d is days since this template was last sent to this user. Chosen values: "γ = 0.017 and h = 15" (half-life 15 days). They guessed "a few weeks to a month" and grid searched gamma.
- Choice: softmax over adjusted scores, temperature tau for exploration. Argmax was used offline only.
- History used: recent rounds weighted more. Training offline on 15 days, test 19 days, from a random-send log of 34 days.
- Lift, 2-week A/B (Table 8): DAU +0.5 %, lessons +0.4 %, new user D1 retention +2.2 %, new user D7 +2.0 %, existing user D1 +0.5 %, existing D7 +0.2 % (not significant). Offline predicted a 1.9 % rise in 2-hour conversion. Abstract: "0.5 % increase in total daily active users (DAUs) and a 2 % increase in new user retention".
- Five months later, a 5 percent random holdout: "had 2.5 % higher reward than the holdout set".
- Blog summary, https://blog.duolingo.com/hi-its-duo-the-ai-behind-the-meme/ (3 Sep 2020), CONFIRMED: "it used to be that notifications were selected from the pool at random." Best template also differs by language: "the 'Time for [language]' notification works very well for Chinese learners, but it's usually not the best option for English learners." Data was "~200 million practice reminders sent over a 34-day period".

## 3. Streak-at-risk nudges

- CONFIRMED that the idea exists. https://blog.duolingo.com/how-duolingo-streak-builds-habit/ (31 Jan 2022): "Even on your laziest days (we all have them!), you'll be reminded to complete a lesson so as not to lose your hard-earned progress." This uses loss aversion.
- Same page: Streak Freeze, up to two equipped. Sentence: "this change actually increased the relative number of active learners on Duolingo every day by +0.38%."
- The send hour and conditions of a late nudge: not published. Gap. Their own tip is a user alarm: "Set an alarm for 11:50pm or near your bedtime." (tips-for-maintaining-streak).
- REPORTED league nudges: "You're out of the top 5" and "Last chance to get promoted!" Source: https://duolingo.deconstructoroffun.com/mechanics/leagues

## 4. Backoff when ignored

No Duolingo primary source. All REPORTED.
- Podcast summary above: "daily reminders for up to seven days, stopping when effectiveness drops." Also "most users don't come back after a week" and best win-back is "within the first three to four days".
- One user's own account (via a web search snippet, not fetched): a "these reminders don't seem to be working" email on day 8, pushes stopped, a second similar email on day 18, pauses of 3 days then 1 day. A single anecdote, not policy.
- Resurrected user defined as 30 days inactive: https://blog.duolingo.com/back-from-the-brink-what-duolingo-learned-about-its-resurrected-users (seen in search only).
- Same podcast summary: for long-gone users, "social reactivation beats app-driven outreach". Friends bringing friends back beats app reminders.

## 6. Leagues, friend features, freezes

- Leagues, CONFIRMED, https://blog.duolingo.com/duolingo-leagues-leaderboards/ (3 May 2023): "Every Sunday (based on your timezone), a new weekly league begins." "each week, you're matched with people who have similar study habits to you and who are in a similar time zone." Ten leagues today, first tested 2018 with 5. "The top 10 learners in the Diamond league are eligible for entry into the Diamond Tournament." Cheaters with "illegitimate XP gains" are removed.
- Size 30 and promotion counts (Bronze top 20 down to top 5 in Obsidian): REPORTED by community guides in a search snippet only. A teardown (https://duolingo.deconstructoroffun.com/mechanics/leagues) gives shares instead: Bronze about 67 %, Gold 33 %, Sapphire 23 %, Obsidian 17 %, Diamond none. It says results land on Monday, which conflicts with the official Sunday. Demotion of the bottom slice is REPORTED. Exact demotion counts: not found.
- Friend quests, CONFIRMED, https://blog.duolingo.com/friends-quests/ (9 Sep 2022): weekly random pairing with a mutual follower, after 1 p.m. EST Tuesday, only if you did "at least one lesson for the week". "you have five days to complete the challenge". Only active friends are paired. Prewritten "nudges" can be sent. "learners who follow friends are 5.6x more likely to finish their language course."
- Friend streaks, REPORTED, https://duolingo.deconstructoroffun.com/mechanics/streaks : "Users with at least one Friend Streak are 22% more likely to complete their daily lesson". Up to five friend streaks. A nudge button appears after you finish your lesson.
- Freeze, CONFIRMED: up to two equipped at once (blog above). Earn Back (do a few lessons after a break to restore): REPORTED. Freezes are applied silently, REPORTED. Perfect Streak (no freezes) as a prestige tier, REPORTED.
- Low bar, CONFIRMED, https://blog.duolingo.com/improving-the-streak/ (19 Nov 2020): streak now needs "just a single lesson each day". Result: "3.3% increase in Day 14 retention", "a 1% increase in our overall daily active learners", "10.5% increase in the percentage of daily learners on a streak" in 20 days.

## 7. Streak widget

CONFIRMED, https://blog.duolingo.com/widget-feature/ (29 Aug 2023):
- Shows only two facts: "whether or not they had done a lesson that day, and the length of their current streak." Tests found that "simply reminding a learner of their current streak, and whether or not it's at risk, is hugely valuable."
- Mood by time: "Duo's mood at different parts of the day, depending on whether or not you'd done your lesson. You'll see he gets more and more desperate as it nears midnight!" Done today shows a relaxed, happy Duo. Not done shows increasingly panicked.
- Launched on iOS July 2022. "half of the learners with the widget installed have a streak of at least 6 months" (selection effect). "learners using the widget had far better retention ... even when controlling for" commitment.
- Refresh rate: not published. Gap. (iOS widgets refresh on a system budget, not Duolingo's choice.)

## 8. Numbers that say what worked

All CONFIRMED unless marked.
- Bandit: DAU +0.5 %, new user D7 +2.0 %, holdout +2.5 % after 5 months (KDD paper).
- Single lesson counts for streak: D14 +3.3 %, DAU +1 % (improving-the-streak).
- Two freezes: DAU +0.38 % (how-duolingo-streak-builds-habit).
- Streak animation: new learner day 7 retention +1.7 % (same page).
- 7 day streak: "2.4 times more likely to continue using Duolingo the next day" (improving-the-streak); "3.6 times more likely to complete their course" (streak-builds-habit).
- Friends: 5.6x course completion (friends-quests). Friend streak 22 % more likely to do the daily lesson (REPORTED).

## What Saulog OS should copy

Verdict key: CONFIRMS, CHANGES, CONTRADICTS, UNCONFIRMED (no source either way).

1. One reminder per goal per day at the usual time. CONFIRMS the idea, CHANGES the method. Only one source (REPORTED) gives a rule: 23.5 hours after last activity. Use: median of last 42 days when there are 5 or more logs, else the last log time plus 23.5 h, else the plan's defaults. No-history behaviour is unpublished, so defaults stay as written.
2. Message rotation and bandit. CONFIRMS. Use score s* = s - 0.017 * 0.5^(days since last used / 15). Reward = a log within 2 h of the send. Pick with softmax, not argmax. CHANGES for scale: with under 10 people the data is far too thin (Duolingo had millions, and small samples gave scores off by more than 5 % in 38 % of arms). Start the score at 0 for every template, shrink hard toward 0 (for example add 20 pseudo sends at the group average), and keep the recency penalty doing most of the work. Eligibility rules per template (needs a streak, needs a goal) are a good copy.
3. Late streak-at-risk nudge, 3 h before midnight. UNCONFIRMED on the hour. The idea is CONFIRMED (loss aversion, "11:50pm" alarm tip, widget panic). Keep about 21:00 as our own pick. Send only if nothing is logged and the streak is 2 or more days (a day 1 streak has little to lose).
4. Backoff at 7 days halve, 14 days stop. CHANGES. The only reported pattern is daily reminders for up to 7 days, a "don't seem to be working" message about day 8, and a second message about day 18. Halving is not reported. Suggested numbers: after 7 days with no log, send one goodbye message and drop to one nudge every 3 days; after 18 days, stop fully until the next log. Mark this as REPORTED, not confirmed.
5. Hard caps 3 a day, 22:30 to 07:30, 90 min gap. UNCONFIRMED (Duolingo publishes none). Keep as is. They are consistent with 1 reminder per goal plus 1 late nudge plus 1 friend note.
6. Friend note once a day per friend. CONFIRMS in spirit. Duolingo gates friend nudges behind your own finished lesson and they are one tap with prewritten text. Copy: only the sender chooses, from 3 to 5 fixed messages, once a day per friend, never in quiet hours. Do not auto-send "Ana just finished Upper" without the friend being able to turn it off.
7. Levels and streaks section.
   - Streak needs one log a day: CONFIRMS (single lesson, D14 +3.3 %). Do not tie streak to a calorie or protein goal.
   - Freezes: CONFIRMS a cap of 2 equipped. Apply them silently, no confirm dialog.
   - Weekly crew table Mon to Sun, no tiers: CONFIRMS. Duolingo uses about 30 per league (REPORTED) and 10 tiers, so tiers make no sense for under 10. Official reset is Sunday local time, so Mon to Sun with a Sunday night cut is fine. CHANGES: also show a small shared weekly goal like Friends Quest (one goal for the pair, 5 days, shown from Tuesday) because that is the confirmed pairing feature.
   - Friend streak: CONFIRMS, with 22 % lift (REPORTED). Cap at 5 friend streaks per person, which only matters if the crew grows.
   - Crew crown: no source. Neutral.
   - Daily XP caps: no source. Neutral. Duolingo removes illegitimate XP, so server checks are needed.
8. Widget flame states: CONFIRMS. Two facts only: streak length and done or not done today. Mood changes by time of day when not done. Use the plan's 18:00 as the first "worried" step, then more worried near 21:00 and 23:00. Done today shows calm. Frozen is our own extra state. Refresh: set the Scriptable refresh to about 15 to 30 min and also refresh after each log. Duolingo does not publish its rate.
9. Always log every send and open (notification_events). CONFIRMS: the reward needs sends joined to a log within 2 h.
10. Expected size of the win. Duolingo saw DAU +0.5 % from message choice and +2 % new user retention. For friends, do not expect a clean A/B. Treat the numbers as direction only.
